# STATE_2026-06-10.md

A snapshot of what The Land's simulation actually does, intended for the next
person (human or agent) picking it up. Sectioned and pitched to match the
"Sim model essentials" section of CLAUDE.md, but with more detail because a
lot has accreted that CLAUDE.md doesn't mention.

## File layout — what's actually there

- `src/biomes.ts` (78 LOC) — terrain generation. Pure, deterministic.
- `src/iso.ts` (149 LOC) — iso projection + diamond/overlay primitives + HSL helpers.
- `src/sim.ts` (1375 LOC) — the simulation. No Pixi imports. Owns `SimWorld`, `Civ`, cities, expeditions, catastrophes.
- `src/names.ts` (81 LOC) — per-era syllable pools, `generateName` + `evolveName`.
- `src/main.ts` (1527 LOC) — Pixi bootstrap, six-layer scene, tick loop, animation systems, HUD, civ bars, event log, building sprites.
- `src/counter.ts`, `src/style.css`, `src/assets/` — Vite scaffolding leftovers; not in use.

## Terrain (`biomes.ts`)

- `generateBiomeMap(w, h, seed)` returns `{ biomes: Biome[][], elevation: number[][] }`. Both grids are needed downstream because catastrophes (flood, earthquake) mutate elevation and re-derive biome.
- **Three** seeded simplex-noise channels, not two:
  - continental (low frequency, `CONTINENTAL_SCALE = 0.025`, weight 0.65)
  - detail (higher frequency, `DETAIL_SCALE = 0.09`, weight 0.35)
  - moisture (`MOISTURE_SCALE = 0.09`)
- Elevation = `continental * 0.65 + detail * 0.35`. Biome classification: `< SEA_LEVEL (0.05)` → water; `< SHORE_LEVEL (0.18)` → sand; `> MOUNTAIN_LEVEL (0.65)` → rock; otherwise moisture picks among forest/grass/fertile.
- PRNG is `mulberry32(seedString + ':channel')`.

## Sim model essentials (current)

- World is `SimTile[][]` (96×96) plus a `Map<civId, Civ>`, an `Expedition[]` queue, a `NameMemory[]`, and two catastrophe counters (`catastrophePressure`, `lastCatastropheTick`). Tile state: `wild | cleared | built | ruin`; `ruin` also carries `ruinEra`.
- Civs are agents with `constitution` (0.6–1.2), `fortune` (drifts ±0.35), `vitality` (eases toward a phase target × constitution), `phase` (`rising → stable → declining → dead`), `era` (one of six), an `maxSize` ambition cap (`SIM.minAmbition..maxAmbition` skewed by `ambitionSkew=2.5`), and a `cities: CivCity[]` list. `cities[0]` is the capital; `originRow/originCol` shadow the capital's position and can move (see capital succession).
- `step()` returns `{ changes, events, biomeChanges }` — three lists the renderer consumes. It reads from an immutable `snapshot` of `{state, civId}` taken at the top of the tick to avoid order-dependence within a tick.
- Effective strength = `vitality + fortune`; effective decay pressure = `clamp(1 - vitality - fortune)`. Both drive spread, conquest, and decay rolls.
- Decay candidates are collected per civ across the grid scan and then capped at `SIM.maxDecaysPerCivPerTick = 1`, sorted by severity. Edge tiles factor in exposure (out-of-bounds counts as exposure), isolation (no same-civ neighbor → up to 5× multiplier, damped near the capital), and distance from the capital (core protected, periphery quadratically more decay-prone). The capital tile itself is protected as long as the civ has > 1 tile.
- Tile spread (`SIM.spreadBase * effectiveStrength`) into wild neighbors; spread into ruin is at half rate. Conquest into a foreign `built` tile happens when `strengthRatio > 1.15`, scaled by defender's periphery distance; on success, 60% chance the tile flips to `cleared` (occupied), 40% to `ruin` carrying the defender's era.
- After a civ enters `dead`, decay continues at a damped rate (`deathDecayMultiplier = 0.4`) but periphery amplification ramps up over `deathPeripheryRampTicks = 600` ticks — dead civs crumble from the edge inward.
- Eras (`ERAS_ORDERED`): `neolithic → classical → medieval → industrial → modern → post`. Era is set at civ birth from `inheritedEraFor(row,col)`: count ruin tiles within `eraInheritanceRadius = 6`; if ≥ `eraInheritanceThreshold = 3` of any era present, that era is inherited, with `eraAdvanceChance = 0.25` of bumping up one era. Civs never advance era while alive — era is inherited at birth, full stop.
- Ruins reclaim to wild at age > `ruinReclaimTicks = 2000` with `ruinReclaimChance = 0.005` per tick.
- Civ spawning: while `livingCivCount < SIM.maxLivingCivs (10)`, each tick has `baseCivSpawnChance = 0.005` to try `pickCivSpawnTile`. Candidates are non-water `wild|ruin` tiles ≥ `newCivMinDistance = 16` from any living civ's capital, weight-biased toward ruin density (1 + `ruinScore * 3`). 120 attempts per call.
- Initial seeding: `seedInitialCivs(world, biomes, 1)` — exactly one civ at world start. Both `resetWorld` and `resetSimOnly` seed 1.

## Cities (CivCity)

A second tier of structure CLAUDE.md doesn't cover. A civ has a list of cities; each city has `{row, col, prominence (0..1), name, foundedTick}`. `cities[0]` is the capital. Cities matter because:

- **Building density** halos around cities, scaled by city `prominence` and civ vitality.
- **Decay distance** is from the nearest city (`nearestCityDist`), not just the capital.
- **Expeditions** can only launch from coast within a city's halo.
- **Capital succession**: when the capital tile is lost, the most prominent surviving city is promoted; `originRow/originCol` are reassigned; a `capital_moved` event fires.

City lifecycle, all running every `CITY.foundingCheckInterval = 20` ticks:

1. `reconcileCities` — drop cities whose tile is no longer civ-owned. Fires `city_fell` events for the capital always, and for non-capitals only when `prominence ≥ CITY.cityFallNarrateThreshold (0.35)`.
2. `updateCityProminence` — each city grows at `prominenceGrowthRate (0.002)` per pass, multiplied by a density bonus from same-civ tile fraction within `prominenceDensityRadius = 8`. Capped at 1.0.
3. `maybefoundCities` — civ earns city slots at `count / CITY.tilesPerCity (80)`, scaled by ambition. A new city is placed on the civ-owned tile farthest from existing cities, requiring `minDistBetweenCities = 14`.

City markers (capitals as big white-ringed dots, others smaller) and city name labels (above `nameLabelThreshold = 0.5` prominence) are drawn in a dedicated `cityMarkersContainer` and rebuilt every `foundingCheckInterval` ticks or on births/catastrophes.

## Expeditions / colonization

- `maybeLaunchExpeditions` per tick: civ needs `effectiveStrength ≥ expeditionMinVitality (0.5)`, `tileCount ≥ expeditionMinSize (18)`, no in-flight expedition, and a 5% roll.
- Launch tile must be a `built` coastal tile within `expeditionLaunchCityRadius (11) × city.prominence` of some city — keeps ships from sailing out of empty/ruined coast. Direction = average of water-neighbor unit vectors.
- Each tick the expedition advances `expeditionSpeed = 0.23` tiles, dies at age > `expeditionMaxAge = 480` or via per-tick loss `expeditionLossBase = 0.001 * (1 + age/30)`.
- On hitting land, looks at the landing tile and four cardinal inland neighbors; the first that isn't water and is `wild|ruin` becomes `cleared` and owned by the civ — a `colony_founded` event fires. Trail (last 12 positions) is rendered as fading dots.

## Breakaways

- Every 15 ticks (`world.tick % 15 === 0`) `maybeBreakaway` runs flood-fill `findCivClusters` for each living civ. If a civ has ≥ 2 clusters and the non-mainland exclave has ≥ `breakawayMinSize (30)` tiles, roll `breakawayChance (0.01)` (× `breakawayWeakParentBonus = 3.0` if parent's effectiveStrength < 0.5).
- New civ inherits parent's era and is born `rising`, vitality 0.5. Name is `evolveName(parent.name, parent.era)`. Capital is the exclave tile farthest from the parent's capital. All exclave tiles reassign to the new civ id.

## Catastrophes

Self-firing system, not in CLAUDE.md. Every tick `catastrophePressure` accumulates from a base + weighted settled fraction + average era rank + time-since-last (`CATASTROPHE` block has all the weights). When pressure crosses `pressureFireThreshold (1.0)`, `applyCatastrophe` fires.

- Severity rolled with `Math.pow(random, severitySkew=2)` — skewed toward low. Three tiers: minor < 0.35, moderate, severe ≥ 0.70.
- Type chosen uniformly from `plague | asteroid | flood | earthquake` (25% each).
- Radius is type-dependent (`regionRadius=18`, `floodRadius=22`, `earthquakeRadius=26`) and scales `0.4 + severity*0.6`. Severe plague/asteroid jump to a fixed `severeRadius=32` to outrun the era-inheritance radius.
- Center is picked from a random `built` tile (no built tiles → no catastrophe fires, but pressure still resets).
- "Embers" guarantee: civs whose capital sits outside the radius are safe; the system pads safe set to `emberCount = 2` by adding civs with fewest tiles in the blast zone.
- Per type:
  - **plague / asteroid**: built→ruin (era downgrades by `eraDowngradeSteps = 2` on moderate, stays current on minor); ruins also degrade (not on minor); severe wipes tiles to wild and erases `nameMemory` in the radius. Asteroid additionally tapers outside `asteroidCoreRadius (6)` with falloff factor 2.0.
  - **flood**: any land tile in radius with `elevation < SEA_LEVEL + floodDepth*severity (0.12)` becomes water permanently (biome mutated, tile reset to wild). Drowned-zone `nameMemory` is erased.
  - **earthquake**: applies a sine-wave elevation perturbation (`amplitude=0.15`, `freq=0.4`, falloff with distance). Land may sink below SEA_LEVEL (drown) or sea floor rise above SHORE_LEVEL (become sand/grass). Biome changes are reported in `biomeChanges`.
- Civs that lost tiles take a vitality hit (`vitalityHit=0.65 × (0.3+severity*0.7)`) and if `rising|stable`, get pushed to `declining`. A `catastrophe` event is emitted with center, severity, type, affectedCivIds; `reconcileCities` is then called.

## Naming

- `nameForNewCiv` searches `nameMemory` within `nameMemoryRadius = 8` of the new civ's capital. If found: if same era, reuse the name; otherwise `evolveName(oldName, newEra)`. Memory is updated in place. If no match, fresh `generateName(era)`.
- `evolveName` keeps a 2–4 letter prefix root and applies one of three strategies (root+suffix, root+part+suffix, or `New/Old/Greater/High` prefix).
- New cities use the same `nameForNewCiv` path, so a founded city in a haunted region can inherit a local name.

## Rendering pipeline (`main.ts`)

Six containers, drawn back-to-front: `biomeLayer → simLayer (state overlays) → buildingLayer → expeditionLayer → cityMarkersContainer → labelLayer`.

Tick loop runs at `ticksPerSecond = 30` via a `deltaMS` accumulator inside `app.ticker.add`. Each frame may step multiple sim ticks. For each tick's `changes`: `noteTileChange` updates the incremental civ index, `refreshTileOverlay` sets new target color/alpha on the persistent per-tile `Graphics`, `refreshBuildingSprite` updates the per-tile building state. For each tick's `biomeChanges`: `refreshBiomeTile` queues a biome color transition.

Animation systems (all running every frame, independently):

- **State overlays** (`animatingTiles`) — lerp color, alpha, border color/alpha/width toward targets at `EASE = 0.15`; destroyed when alpha reaches 0. Borders are era-dependent (heavier in industrial/modern/post). Tile fill alpha multiplied by `TILE_TINT_OPACITY = 0.5`.
- **Biome tiles** (`animatingBiomeTiles`) — for flood/earthquake biome shifts; ease at `BIOME_EASE = 0.06`.
- **Building sprites** (`animatingBuildingTiles`) — per-slot floor1 / mid-floors / roof. Density-driven count (0–4 slots), era-driven floor heights, ruin-decay tinting.
- **Civ labels** (`civLabels`) — name floats at the civ's centroid (computed from `civStats.centSumX/Y`), opacity scales with tile count, font size scales with tile count, dropped when civ dies.
- **Per-civ era saturation easing** (`civCurSatMult`) — building tints transition smoothly when a civ's era changes (currently only via breakaway successor / inheritance at birth — no in-life era advancement exists, so this is mainly for the cataclysm/reset transitions).
- **Dead-civ repaint guard** (`fadedDeadCivs`) — once per civ death, all its owned tiles are explicitly re-overlayed and re-spritedown to RUIN_TINT, because the per-tile `changes` list won't include them (their `state` didn't change).
- **Expeditions** — drawn every frame from scratch into one shared `Graphics`. Trail dots + a diamond marker at the head.

Buildings (`SHOW_BUILDING_SPRITES = true`):

- 8 body textures + 8 roof textures loaded from `/sprites/buildings/buildingTiles_*.png`. Bodies and roofs are split into a "SIMPLE" pool (frames 0–3, for fill-order 0–1 hinterland) and a "GRAND" pool (frames 4–7, for fill-order 2–3 city core).
- Each tile has 4 slots in a deterministic fill order (`tileSlotPermutation` seeded by `(row,col)`), positioned at the bottom vertices of the quadrant sub-tiles. Slot-1 (front) matches the legacy single-building position.
- Building count per tile is bucketed from a 0..1 density score: thresholds 0.20 / 0.40 / 0.60 / 0.80 → 0..4 buildings. Density = `proxFactor × (vitalityBase + (1 - vitalityBase) × civ.vitality)`, where `proxFactor` falls off from the nearest city (effective radius = `DENSITY.proximityScale (11) × max(0.2, prominence)` with `falloffPower = 2.5`).
- Per-building height: floors interpolate within the era's `ERA_HEIGHT_RANGE` (neolithic `[1,1]` → post `[1,6]`) by density, with `±0.6` stable per-slot noise. Implemented as floor1 + N mid-floors + roof, each mid-floor a sprite stacked by `BODY_OFFSET ≈ 4.2px`. Roof Y eases on era change (`ROOF_EASE = 0.10`); mid-floor alpha eases at `MID_FLOOR_EASE = 0.07`.
- Building tint: civ base color, nudged ±10% L / ±18% S deterministically per slot, multiplied by `ERA_SAT_MULT` (0.30 neolithic → 1.40 post), the era saturation eased per-civ at `ERA_SAT_EASE = 0.04`.
- Slot ruin/abandonment: when density drops past a slot, that slot snaps to `RUIN_TINT = 0x5a544c` at 35% opacity and decays toward 0 at `RUIN_DECAY_EASE = 0.00167` per frame (~real-time crumbling), while tinting toward the biome color underneath. When alpha < 0.01, slot sprites are destroyed.
- Periodic density refresh every `DENSITY.refreshInterval = 15` ticks walks the civ index (owned tile sets) to pick up slow vitality/prominence drift.

Performance index (`civStats`, `civTiles`, `tileCivIdSnapshot`): incremental per-tile counters + per-civ tile-key set, updated by `noteTileChange` on every reported change. Used by label centroid calc, dead-civ tile walks, era-saturation tile walks, density refresh, and bar panel — eliminates the full 96×96 grid scans that used to be hot.

## HUD, event log, controls

Top-left HUD: seed, reroll, reset sim, pause, catastrophe (manual trigger), skip 5k, tick, civs alive/total, era summary, expedition count. Bottom-right: civ bar panel — one row per living civ with name, era dot, phase glyph (`▲ ■ ▼ ·`), and a tile-count bar. DOM rebuild throttled to every 10 frames (`BARS_REFRESH_FRAMES`). Bottom-left: event log — narrated strings from `narrateEvent` (era-flavored civ births, declines, deaths, colonies, breakaways, city falls, capital moves, catastrophes). 5 lines max, 22s lifetime with fade after 13s.

Seed persistence: URL `?seed=` → localStorage `theLand:seed` → random. `saveSeed` writes both. URL stays in sync on reroll. "Reroll" regenerates terrain + sim; "Reset sim" keeps terrain; both seed one initial civ.

Skip 5k: runs 5000 sim ticks with the renderer paused, then rebuilds all visual layers from scratch (biomes too, since catastrophes may have mutated terrain).

Auto-cataclysm: every `CATACLYSM_INTERVAL = 100000` ticks the world is automatically rerolled with a fresh random seed and a flavored narration line is pushed to the event log.

## Drift from CLAUDE.md

Items where CLAUDE.md no longer matches the code:

1. **Terrain channels**. CLAUDE.md: "Two simplex-noise channels (elevation + moisture) seeded from a string via a mulberry32 PRNG, with a radial island-falloff applied to elevation." Reality: three channels (continental + detail + moisture); no radial island-falloff at all — elevation is just the weighted sum of the two noise layers. `generateBiomeMap` also returns `elevation` alongside `biomes`, which CLAUDE.md doesn't mention.
2. **`step()` return shape**. CLAUDE.md: "advances one tick and returns the changed tiles." Reality: returns `{ changes, events, biomeChanges }` — a tuple the renderer needs all three of. The event channel is the entire narration/log system; `biomeChanges` carries terrain mutations from flood/earthquake.
3. **Scene layers**. CLAUDE.md: "two-layer scene (biomeLayer under simLayer)". Reality: six containers (`biomeLayer → simLayer → buildingLayer → expeditionLayer → cityMarkersContainer → labelLayer`).
4. **Cities don't exist in CLAUDE.md**. The whole `CivCity` tier, `cities[0]`-as-capital, capital succession via `capital_moved`, prominence-driven markers/labels, city-distance-driven decay (`nearestCityDist` not just capital distance), and the `CITY` constants block are all missing from CLAUDE.md's "Sim model essentials".
5. **Catastrophes don't exist in CLAUDE.md**. Pressure-driven self-firing system, four catastrophe types (plague/asteroid/flood/earthquake), terrain mutation, ember protection, severity tiers, manual catastrophe button.
6. **Auto-cataclysm**. Every 100k ticks `resetWorld(randomSeed())` is called automatically. CLAUDE.md is silent on this.
7. **Building sprites**. The entire `SHOW_BUILDING_SPRITES` pipeline — 4 slots per tile, density-driven count, era-driven heights, ruin decay tinting, era saturation easing — is invisible to CLAUDE.md, which says "smooth tile transitions" but nothing about sprites.
8. **Civ labels, civ bar panel, event log**. Three significant HUD elements not mentioned.
9. **Initial civ count**. CLAUDE.md doesn't specify; current behavior is `seedInitialCivs(world, biomes, 1)` — exactly one starting civ.
10. **`SimWorld` shape**. CLAUDE.md: "tiles + civs + expeditions". Reality also has `nameMemory`, `catastrophePressure`, `lastCatastropheTick`, `nextCivId`, `tick`.
11. **`Civ` shape**. CLAUDE.md lists `constitution, fortune, vitality, phase, era, maxSize`. Reality also has `cities`, `birthTick`, `phaseAge`, `color`, `name`, `id`, `originRow/originCol` (which CLAUDE.md mentions separately under decay).
12. **Constants split**. CLAUDE.md: "All balance constants live in the `SIM` object". Reality: tunables are now spread across `SIM`, `CATASTROPHE`, and `CITY` — plus a stack of render-side constants in `main.ts` (`DENSITY`, `ERA_HEIGHT_RANGE`, `ERA_SAT_MULT`, `LABEL`, `ERA_TINT`, `RUIN_*`). Tuning a "civ" knob is still cheap; tuning a "look" knob means crossing the sim/render boundary.
13. **Skip / catastrophe buttons**. HUD now has buttons CLAUDE.md doesn't mention.

What CLAUDE.md still gets right and is worth preserving:

- The fixed-tickrate accumulator, the snapshot-before-mutate pattern in `step()`, the `fadedDeadCivs` guard, `maxDecaysPerCivPerTick` decay capping, capital protection while civ has > 1 tile, breakaway flood-fill being throttled to every 15 ticks, the era-inheritance mechanic, the name-memory radius, seed persistence round-trip, and the "no Pixi imports in sim.ts" rule.
