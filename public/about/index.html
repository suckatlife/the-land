About the project

A world that carries on without you.

The Land is a deep-time diorama. Leave it running, or watch it closely; it does not behave differently either way.

Civilizations settle, cross oceans, make war, build wonders, and disappear. Weather moves through. Ruins remain. Then the world begins again.

There is nothing to win and no right way to watch. Leave it running, lean in when something changes, or share a seed so someone else can see the same land.

Keep it around

You can install The Land from your browser's menu. It opens in its own window and, after the first visit, can reopen without a connection.
